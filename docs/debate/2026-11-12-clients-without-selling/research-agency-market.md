## 1. India: agency-to-partner prices
- No public Indian wholesale price list was found. Two examples:
  - 24siteshop (Guwahati) partner programme: illustrative partner base Rs 10,000, agency quotes the client Rs 20,000; the partner never contacts the client (24siteshop.com/partner-program, undated, (c) 2026, "illustrative").
  - Grovia white-label WordPress: Starter (up to 5 pages, 2 revisions) Rs 84,416; Business (up to 15 pages, WooCommerce-ready) Rs 2,11,166 (groviaos.com/services/whitelabel-wordpress, undated).
- What agencies charge end clients, Surat: WordPress Rs 14,000-48,000; e-commerce Rs 40,000-2,50,000; clinic / coaching / CA sites Rs 20,000-50,000; IndiaMART local vendors Rs 7,000-10,000 (codingclave.com/cost/website-development-cost-in-surat, "checked 4 Oct 2026"; a Lucknow firm's own page).
- Indian wholesale price for small e-commerce: not found. Freelancer 5-7 page WordPress Rs 3,000-15,000: unverified.

## 2. White-label for US / UK / AU agencies (offshore partner prices)
- Codingclave (Lucknow), 2026-10-25: WordPress from the agency's design $200-800; custom-designed WordPress or Next.js $300-1,200; store $600-3,500; care plans $75 / 150 / 300 per site per month; 40/40/20 payments; code ownership on final payment; 6 months free bug fixes (codingclave.com/usa/services/white-label-web-development).
- PageTraffic: 5-page business site from $799, e-commerce from $1,499, maintenance $55/month (pagetraffic.com/white-label-design-development.php, undated).
- Second-hand from Codingclave's comparison: E2M (Ahmedabad) from $1,299/month for 30-35 hours; The White Label Agency $1,499-4,699; WP Buffs $89/site/month (own pages returned 403; unconfirmed).
- Agency resale (vendor blogs): 5-page WordPress costs the agency $800-2,500, sells for $1,600-5,000 (agencyplatform.com/blog/?p=4014, 2026-06-29); $800-2,500 cost, $2,500-7,500 price, 50-65 % margin; India senior developers $18-45/hour vs US $125-300/hour (krishaweb.com/blog/white-label-web-development-pricing, 2026-05-14).

## 3. How white-label partners find agencies; failures; protections (vendor claims)
- Gatilab (gatilab.com/white-label-wordpress-development, 2026-08-23): channels Clutch (agencies with 2-50 staff), Dribbble, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, WordCamps (one talk = 2 long-term partners), referrals; cold email "a grind"; referrals close 3-4x cold; partner referrals ~50 %; start with 1 partner for 90 days; a project manager at 3-5 partners; example 3 partners at $5,000-8,000/month each; agencies expect 40-60 % below retail; scope creep 2-5 % of time vs 15-20 % on direct clients.
- CODERCOPS (blog.codercops.com/blog/white-label-agency-partnerships-2026, 2026-05-24): partners from meetups, mutual-contact referrals, people who saw the work; net-15 / net-30 payment; agency markup 20-50 %; failures: late payment, quality disputes, thin margins.
- Protections: staged payments 40/40/20 (Codingclave) or client 50/30/20 vs partner 30/40/30 (abbacustechnologies.com, about Oct 2026); NDA + no-approach of the agency's clients, client contact only with agency approval, code ownership on full payment, written change orders, staging site (Gatilab); review checkpoints at 30/60/90 % (CODERCOPS).
- Unverified (Act not opened): MSMED Act s.15 - buyers must pay a Udyam-registered micro/small supplier within 45 days (15 days without a written agreement); Income-tax s.43B(h) delays the buyer's deduction until paid.
- No conversion data found for LinkedIn outreach, Upwork agency contracts or Facebook groups.

## 4. Referral / commission partners
- Mavlers: 20 % of a referred client's first one-off project, 15 % of retainers for 12 months, paid after the client's first payment (mavlers.com/referral-program, undated).
- Cyber Alliance India (updated 2025-12-30): 5 % up to Rs 50,000, 8 % to Rs 3 L, 10 % to Rs 5 L, 12.5 % above; paid after full client payment; lead valid 30 days; TDS s.194H (tax sites: 2 % since 2024-10-01, Rs 20,000 threshold from 2025-04-01 - unverified).
- CA firms: ICAI rules restrict fee-sharing with non-members (unverified whether that blocks a referral fee).
- Commission-only reps: weak sources; base + ~10 % year one suggested (sakasandcompany.com); reps struggle in web/SEO without product knowledge (ukbusinessforums.co.uk).
