## 1. Freelance marketplaces
- Upwork: variable 0-15 % fee per contract since 2025-05-01, fixed when the proposal is sent (gigradar.io/blog/upwork-fees, 2026-08-04).
- Upwork Connects: $0.15 each; 10 free a month on Basic; a proposal costs 6-16 Connects; Freelancer Plus $19.99/month with 100 Connects (gigradar; skydo.com/blog/upwork-fees-freelancers, updated 2026-11-03).
- Upwork rejects profiles with generic skills to balance supply and demand (community.upwork.com thread 498879, 2018). "Over 60 % of Indian applicants rejected in 2025": unverified.
- Upwork withdrawal to an Indian bank: $0.99 (skydo).
- Upwork Project Catalog: fixed-price listings that clients buy (the inbound route; everything else is bidding). Catalog fee: unverified.
- Fiverr: flat 20 % fee; funds clear after 14 days; Payoneer withdrawal $3 (vaultleap.com/blog/fiverr-fees-explained-2026, 2026-07-15).
- Fiverr Level 1: 5 orders, 3 unique clients, $400+ earned, success score 5+ (de.fiverr.com/levels, undated; third-party guides say 10 orders / $500).
- Fiverr: buyers order the gig (inbound). Fiverr "Briefs" match buyers to sellers, but new sellers report getting none (community.fiverr.com thread 345664, undated).
- Contra: 0 % commission for freelancers; clients pay $19 per project, or a scaled $2-29 fee (sources conflict) (contra.com/commission-free, undated).
- Contra Pro: $29/month or $199/year, "boosted visibility in job matching", no platform fees (help.contra.com article 9322981, updated 2026-10-05).
- Toptal: under 3 % of applicants accepted; screening 3-8 weeks with 1-3 week test projects; Toptal matches clients (toptal.com/top-3-percent, undated).
- Arc.dev: advertises "top 2 %"; 2.3 % is a third-party figure (unverified) (arc.dev/employer-blog/vetting-process, undated).
- Freelancer.com: bidding only; fee 10 % or $5 (AU page 11 % / A$5.50); 6 free bids a month (freelancer.com.au/feesandcharges, undated).

## 2. Partner directories that send leads
- Shopify: directory invite-only from the Select tier up; from 2027-01-01 the entry tier is Silver = $25K attributed GMV + 1 attributed shop within 1 year (help.shopify.com partners/directory/join and partner-program-tiers, undated). India eligibility and lead volume unverified.
- Webflow Certified: 3 showcased sites, 400+ points, pre-qualification exam; Premium needs 4,000 points (webflow.com/certified-partners/apply, undated). Listing and matching start at Certified, which needs a paid Freelancer or Agency Workspace (digidop.com/blog/webflow-partner-program, 2026-10-06). Webflow makes "no commitment" on lead numbers.
- Framer Experts: free and instant; needs 2 published Framer sites on custom domains; gives a directory listing, client requests matched by Framer, and up to 50 % commission when a client takes over the project; "Pro Expert" after $500 in such commissions brings "more and faster leads"; no country limits stated (framer.com/help/articles/how-to-become-a-framer-expert, updated 2026-10-02).
- Wix Marketplace: Icon partner level, 20 Premium sites live 30+ days, account 6+ months old, business site on a custom domain; Wix matches leads (support.wix.com article wix-studio-applying-to-join-the-wix-marketplace, undated).
- Codeable: applications closed (waitlist); clients pay a 17.5 % fee; estimates assume $80-120/hour (codeable.io/apply, codeable.io/pricing, 2026).

## 3. Getting paid from abroad as an unregistered individual
- PayPal: 4.4 % + $0.30, about 3 % FX markup, 18 % GST on the fee (winvesta.in, 2026-02-20); purpose code mandatory (paypal.com/in/cshelp HELP978, undated).
- Payoneer: card 2.90 % + $0.49, ACH 1 %, FX to INR 1-4 %, $29.95/year if under $6,000 received in 12 months (gauravtiwari.org/payoneer-india-review, 2026-08-24).
- Wise: Indian receiving is for Business accounts; e-FIRC $2 per transfer (wise.com help 71lNXW0Ls3gEFhUH8PtodV, undated).
- Skydo: $19 per payment up to $2,000, $29 for $2,001-10,000, 0.3 % above, + 18 % GST on the fee; no FX markup; free FIRA (xflowpay.com/blog/skydo-fees, 2026-10-28); personal PAN accepted (skydo.com/help, undated).
- Razorpay: individuals take international payments only via PayPal or international bank transfer; cards need a registered business (razorpay.com/docs/payments/international-payments, undated).
- Stripe India: invite-only since May 2024 (support.stripe.com, undated).
- GST: exports count as inter-state; Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax exempts registration up to Rs 20 lakh aggregate turnover (taxheal.com, secondary source - CA to confirm). LUT = annual Form GST RFD-11 for GST-registered exporters (cleartax.in, undated).

## 4. Indian lead sources where the buyer writes the requirement first
- IndiaMART MDC: Rs 4,000/month, Rs 35,000/year (before tax); BuyLeads Rs 40 / 34 / 27 each (help.indiamart.com MDC page, undated; 2026 price unverified).
- Justdial: starting package about Rs 20,000/year, quoted on a sales call (the-ken.com, undated; unverified).
- Sulekha: price per lead varies, shown in the app after enrolling (sulekha.com/collateral/advertise, undated).
- Google Partners: an Ads-agency badge ($10K spend in 90 days), not a lead channel for web builders (support.google.com answer 9702452, undated).
