# Founder context (written by Claude Code for this round - no customer data)

- Founder in India (Mumbai). Strategy-only conversation: nothing will be built until the founder approves.
- Follow-up to round 1 (2026-11-09, summary attached as previous-RESULT.md). Round 1 ended with two
  open founder decisions. The founder has now answered them:
  1. Consumer (B2C) only. No B2B: the founder has no existing business customers and believes B2B
     marketplaces are already crowded (Amazon, Alibaba, IndiaMART).
  2. No field team today. The founder cannot recruit, train and supervise employed field workers now.
     Aggregating independent providers who already do the work (the way ScrapUncle uses existing local
     kabadiwalas as partner vendors) is acceptable.
- The founder is a total fresher in business: older in age, can do ordinary work,
  but has no startup or finance experience. The founder does not know terms like EBITDA, gross margin or CAC.
  Every number must be calculated and explained step by step, as a mentor with 50 years of experience
  as a CEO / founder would explain to a beginner.
- The founder sees two types of opportunity:
  - Type 1 - management / aggregation layer: an existing supply is organised behind one app with
    fast fulfilment (Zomato: food from restaurants and home kitchens anytime; Blinkit: daily items in
    minutes; OYO: standardised budget rooms with photos; Uber: car hire by distance; Rapido: bikes and
    scooters as taxis, useful for students).
  - Type 2 - an activity done regularly offline that has not come online yet. Example the founder saw on Shark
    Tank India: a Delhi scrap / kabadi pickup app where households book a pickup and local scrap
    dealers with accounts on the app collect it.
- Existing assets (only relevant if they genuinely help): SiteBlitz web studio; an internal tool that
  finds local businesses from open map data; a local dataset of about 86.75 million business places
  (Overture Maps open data) including Indian cities - names, categories, locations, some phone / website.
