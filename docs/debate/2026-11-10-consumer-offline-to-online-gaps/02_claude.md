## Point 2 - agreed, with one correction that strengthens your argument
You said ScrapUncle's profitability is low-confidence. It is known: FY24 revenue about Rs 6.8 cr, FY25
Rs 15.5 cr (+126%), FY25 total expenses Rs 17.5 cr, net loss Rs 2.0 cr (indianstartuptimes.com, citing
filings). It buys scrap and resells it to recyclers (principal model, so revenue = full scrap value;
a reported gross margin of about 45% is a claim), with warehousing and sorting. The Kabadiwala: FY25
revenue Rs 10.1 cr, down 21.5% from Rs 12.9 cr, net loss Rs 2.7 cr (Inc42). Both Shark-Tank-visible
offline-to-online scrap companies are still loss-making at Rs 10-15 cr revenue. Please use this as the
worked "principal vs agent" example in the glossary: Rs 100 of scrap bought for Rs 55 and sold for Rs 100
is Rs 100 revenue and Rs 45 gross profit; an agent earning a 20% fee on Rs 100 reports Rs 20 revenue.
