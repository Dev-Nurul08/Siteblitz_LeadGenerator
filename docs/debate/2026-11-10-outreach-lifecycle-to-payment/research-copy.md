# Claude Code research - cold outreach copy, cadence, unique hooks, localisation, lifecycle (2026-11-10)

Web research by a Claude Code research agent, 55 tool calls. Labels: IND = independent research, regulator,
platform policy or press; VEN = vendor's own data; ANEC = self-reported anecdote; n.d. = page shows no date.

## 1. Cold email benchmarks
- Reply rates depend on the denominator. Belkins (VEN) 7.53 M emails in 2025: 0.45 % unique replies / sent;
  owners 0.57 %, firms with 0-10 staff 0.72 %, Food & Beverage 3.47 %. https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates (2026-06-26)
- Hunter (VEN) 31 M emails 2025: 4.5 % per sequence; 21-50 recipients 6.2 % vs 500+ 2.4 %; custom domain 5.2 % vs
  free webmail 2.5 %; 20-49 sends/day/account 5.7 %. https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email (2026, n.d.)
- Instantly (VEN): average 3.43 %, top 10 % >= 10.7 %. https://instantly.ai/report-page (2026-01-12)
- Follow-ups conflict: Instantly - 58 % of replies from email 1, recommends 4-7 touches 3-4 days apart. Hunter -
  3 messages 6.8 % vs 1 message 3.3 %; 3+ follow-ups worse on average. Belkins (VEN) - a 4th follow-up ~triples
  unsubscribes and >3x spam-mark risk. https://belkins.io/blog/sales-follow-up-statistics (n.d.)
- Length barely matters: 34 M emails, buckets 3.4-4.5 %, best 20-39 words (Hunter VEN, 2024-04-26). Hunter 2025
  recommends 40-60 words plain text; Instantly top performers < 80 words.
- Subject: 5-6 words (Hunter 2026, VEN). Google: no fake "Re:"/"Fwd:", display name must identify the sender.
  https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126 (from 2024-02-01)
- Plain text beats HTML: HubSpot A/B - HTML -25 % opens, GIFs -37 % (2014, VEN).
- Open tracking: 7.4 % replies without vs 4.4 % with (Hunter 2026, VEN, correlation). Lemlist (VEN): +15 % spam placement.
  Link in first email: no data found.
- Personalisation: 2 custom fields 5.6 % vs 3.6 %; hand-edited 5.2 % vs 4.4 % automated (Hunter 2026, VEN);
  71 % of decision-makers: irrelevance is the main reason for no reply (Hunter 2025 survey, VEN).
- Interest CTA ("interested?") books >2x meetings vs asking for time (Gong, VEN, n.d.).

## 2. "Sounds like AI"
- People cannot reliably detect AI text: 6 experiments, 4,600 people, 50-52 % accuracy; flawed cues (contractions,
  first person, family topics read as human). Jakesch, Hancock & Naaman, PNAS 2023-03-07 (IND). https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.07271
- Suspected heavy AI costs trust: ~1,100 professionals; sincerity 40-52 % with heavy AI vs 83 % light AI.
  Cardon & Coman, Int. J. Business Communication (IND). https://news.ufl.edu/2025/08/writing-ai-work/ (2025-08)
- Disclosing AI lowers trust; being exposed lowers it more. 13 experiments, Schilke & Reimann, OBHDP vol 188, 2025-05 (IND).
- 69 % of US decision-makers bothered by AI email unless it feels human (Hunter 2026 survey, VEN).
- Tells (Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing", community guide, IND): hype words (vibrant, testament, nestled), lists of
  three, "not just X but Y", trailing "-ing" clauses, vague attributions, chatbot helper phrases, em dashes (older
  indicator); no single sign proves AI. Overused words "delves", "showcasing", "underscores" (Kobak et al.,
  Science Advances 2025-07-02, IND).
- "I hope this email finds you well" / "I came across your business" as AI tells: no study, practitioner lore.
- No primary Google statement that AI-written text is penalised by spam filters.

## 3. Selling websites to local businesses
- No independent reply / close data for any tactic. Free mockup: one freelancer self-reports 6.2 % reply,
  ~40 % of repliers wanted the mockup, ~25 % of mockups became paid (ANEC).
  Free audit offer 14.7 % vs call ask 11.2 % (VEN case, not web design). Loom/Intercom +19 % replies (VEN).
- AIGA (IND) opposes spec work (quality, IP, fairness). Video/mockups trade volume for reply rate; time cost unmeasured.

## 4. Voice notes and video
- WhatsApp: Meta requires opt-in before a business starts a conversation; unsolicited messages risk blocks and
  suspension. https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/overview/getting-opt-in . Cold voice-note reply data:
  vendor-only, contradictory (15-20 % vs ~4 %).
- Instagram: a non-follower can send one text-only message request; photos, video, voice blocked until accepted
  (Engadget 2023-08-03, IND).
- Cloned voices: 58 % judged human (Lavan et al., PLOS One 2025-09, IND); listeners ~60 % accurate (Barrington,
  Cooper & Farid, Scientific Reports 2025, IND).
- Disclosure: EU AI Act Art. 50 AI-generated audio disclosure applies from 2026-08-02 (Travers Smith summary);
  India IT Amendment Rules 2026 require labelling synthetic audio (notified 2026-02-10, Khaitan & Co summary);
  US FCC 2024-02-08: AI voices = "artificial voice" under TCPA (calls only).
- Agent verdict: no AI-cloned voice in cold outreach; after a reply/opt-in, founder's own real voice note or a
  60-90 s screen recording of their free concept.

## 5. Localisation
- CSA Research 2020-07-07, 8,709 consumers, 29 countries: 76 % prefer own language, 40 % never buy from other-language
  sites, 65 % prefer own language even if quality is poor (consumer, not B2B).
- Machine translation: Google Translate 92 % accurate into Spanish, 81 % into Chinese, with clinically significant
  errors (Khoong et al., JAMA Intern Med 2019-02-25, IND).
- Conventions (language guides): German Sie, "Sehr geehrte Frau X," / "Guten Tag Frau X,", "Mit freundlichen Grüßen";
  French vous, "Bonjour Madame X", "Cordialement"; Spanish usted, "Estimado/a", "Atentamente"; Italian Lei;
  Brazilian Portuguese "Prezado/a" (você vs o senhor disputed - native check); Japanese first contact
  はじめてご連絡いたします, 〇〇様 / ご担当者様, 何卒よろしくお願い申し上げます; Arabic MSA تحية طيبة وبعد; Hindi आप form (no authoritative source).

## 6. No email and no phone
- Google Business Profile chat shut down 2024-07-31 (9to5Google, IND). Meta messaging API cannot start a
  conversation. Contact forms / walk-ins: no data. Postcards: ANA report members-only; secondary sites ~2-5 % for
  bought consumer lists (unverified).
- Germany UWG §7: cold advertising email needs prior consent even B2B (ZoomInfo EU laws PDF 2022, secondary).
  Canada CASL: published address = implied consent only if role-relevant and no "no solicitation" note (CRTC, IND).

## 7. Lifecycle
- Deposit norm 25-50 %, 50/50 common for short projects (practitioner guides, no survey). Contract should state
  whether the deposit is refundable (Sprintlaw AU).
- Typical stages: reply -> brief -> fixed quote -> deposit -> draft -> 1-2 revisions -> balance -> handover of domain
  and logins -> optional hosting/maintenance plan. Breakup-email response claims vendor-only.
