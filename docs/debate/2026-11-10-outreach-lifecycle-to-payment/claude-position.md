# Claude Code's own position before reading ChatGPT round 1 (2026-11-10, written while the reply was pending)

Sources: founder-context.md (measured data), research-copy.md, research-legal.md, SiteBlitz `offer.ts`,
SiteBlitz debate 2026-11-01 RESULT (3 qualifying questions, "Review before you pay", industry demos),
lead generator `country_rules.py`.

1. **Hygiene.** Nothing in the world folder is deleted; outreach uses a filtered list. Keep: open, no own website,
   not a brand/chain, category that buys websites, country allowed for the channel. Email ladder: guessed by our
   tool -> never send; bad syntax / no MX -> drop email; same address on >= 5 places -> not the owner, drop;
   verifier ok -> send; catch-all -> only after the ok pool, <= 10 % of a day; unknown -> re-check in 72 h; invalid
   -> route to another channel; hard bounce / "no" -> suppress forever on every channel. Gmail/Yahoo addresses are
   normal for small businesses (79 % in India) and stay.
2. **Routing.** Email first where the law allows. Phone-only: no cold WhatsApp (policy needs number given + opt-in),
   no cold calls/SMS from a 10-digit SIM in India (TRAI 2025 penalties). Instead: find a published email on their
   social page, Mumbai walk-ins, a posted card with their concept + QR that opens WhatsApp so they message first,
   Click-to-WhatsApp ads. Social-only: at most one manual text request, low priority. Nothing: drop.
3. **Email.** 50-90 words, plain text, no link / image / attachment / tracking pixel in touch 1, subject 2-5 plain
   words, first line = a checkable fact from our data (they have Instagram/Facebook, no website), honest "small new
   studio in Mumbai", offer = free homepage concept, CTA = an interest question, real name + phone, one-line
   opt-out, postal address where the law needs it. Human = specific and plain: no "hope this finds you well",
   no "came across", no hype words, no em dashes, no lists of three; templates written once per language by a
   human and reviewed, only fields filled per lead - no per-email AI text.
4. **Lifecycle.** 3 touches (day 0, day 4, day 10 breakup with the industry demo link) - matches the agreed
   1,000 x 3 pilot and the evidence that touch 4+ raises spam marks. Reply within 2 business hours. Free = one
   homepage concept only. Deposit (Rs 4,000 / 9,000) after written agreement and before any build work; balance
   (Rs 3,999 / 8,999) after preview approval and before going live / handing over logins. After launch: review
   request, permission for the first real case study, referral ask, care-plan offer (no price exists yet).
5. **Countries.** Pilot India in English. Email-go later: US, Canada (role-relevant), Australia, UK Ltd/LLP,
   France (profession-related; generic info@ addresses outside the consent rules). Hold: DE, AT, GR, NL, ES, ZA, AE,
   UK sole traders, IT and SG until checked. Language by country with region overrides (Quebec FR, Belgium, Swiss
   cantons); formal register; one native review per language. Non-India price: founder decision; recommend India
   only until 3 paid clients.
6. **Unique hook.** Reject an AI-cloned voice in cold outreach (no evidence of lift, WhatsApp cannot carry audio
   cold, EU AI Act Art. 50 and India IT Rules 2026 labelling, trust loss when discovered). The "wow" is their own
   homepage concept on a private link, shown in a 60-90 s screen recording in the founder's real voice, after
   they say yes; for Indian phone-only shops a printed card of that concept with a QR to WhatsApp.
7. **Pilot.** India, no-website businesses with verified email, verticals that already have demos (salon & beauty,
   coaching & tuition, retail & showroom); 1,000 verified x 3 touches after the 3-week warm-up; plus Mumbai walk-ins
   and a small posted-card test. Gates from 2026-11-09 plus concept -> deposit conversion.
