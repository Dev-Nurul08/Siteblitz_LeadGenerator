# Topic

The founder's words (translated from Hinglish): "You and ChatGPT have to find gaps that are not available
online. For example, Zomato solved the problem that we can order food anywhere, anytime. There are similar
problems that you and ChatGPT must find and list, and how we can override them - just like apps such as
Zomato, OYO, Uber and Rapido did. We have to do the same."

## Agenda
1. Rubric: agree the scoring rubric (frequency, intensity, reach, supply fragmentation / trust, willingness to pay, defensibility, speed to test) before ranking anything.
2. Long list: at least 15 everyday pains in India that are still handled offline or by failing apps - challenge, cut or extend Claude's 14 seed hypotheses below.
3. Proof of "unsolved": for each surviving gap, who already tried it in India (live / pivoted / dead, with source) and the structural reason they have not won.
4. Top 5: score and rank; for each the person with the pain, product, first wedge (city + segment), supply acquisition, revenue model, biggest risk.
5. How to beat incumbents: which proven lever (aggregation, standardisation, trust layer, price transparency, instant availability, city density, cheaper supply model, ignored wedge) applies to each top-5 idea, and why it would work now.
6. Founder fit and 30-day tests: which top-5 idea this small India team can start first, and the one cheap test (with a pass / fail number) that proves or kills each.
7. Graveyard: ideas that look attractive but are traps (already won by a giant, broken unit economics, or regulatory wall), with the reason.

## Claude Code's seed hypotheses (unverified - attack them)
Each line: the pain / what people do today / players I believe exist (status not yet verified by me).

1. Sarkari kaam runner - income / caste / domicile certificates, property mutation, RTO transfers, ration card, succession paperwork. Today: touts and agents, Common Service Centres, self-serve portals (DigiLocker, UMANG). Business-filing sites (Vakilsearch, IndiaFilings) do companies, not citizens.
2. Parents living alone - elderly parents in Tier-2/3 towns while children work in metros or abroad: hospital accompaniment, check-in visits, bank and bill help, emergencies. Players I know of: Emoha, Samarth, Khyaal, Primus - thin coverage, premium pricing.
3. Hospital bills and health-insurance claims - surgery price opacity, cashless claim rejections, TPA delays. Today: the family fights alone. Players: Insurance Samadhan, Policybazaar claim help, Pristyn Care (runs its own surgeries).
4. Daily-wage labour booking - construction helpers, painters, loaders hired at the morning "labour chowk". Today: no identity, no payment guarantee either side. Players: Apna and WorkIndia are job boards, not same-day gigs.
5. Trusted car / bike mechanic - fixed price, genuine parts, warranty. GoMechanic's 2023 troubles left a hole; authorised service centres are expensive; local garages are untrusted.
6. Small home repair and renovation - seepage, re-plumbing, tiling, repainting one room, with a fixed quote and warranty. Livspace / HomeLane target new interiors; Urban Company targets small single-visit jobs; the middle is a contractor you cannot trust.
7. Intercity same-day personal parcel - keys, medicines, documents, home food to family in another city today. Today: informal bus / train parcel counters. Couriers are next-day; Porter is intra-city.
8. Bachelor / migrant renting - deposit not returned, broker fees, PG quality lies. Players: NoBroker, Housing.com, Stanza Living, Zolo. Possibly already solved - graveyard candidate.
9. Water in water-stressed cities - tanker price spikes, unreliable tanker operators, borewell and RO servicing. Fragmented local supply; a city utility may have launched its own tanker booking (to verify).
10. Death and last rites - funeral arrangement plus the paperwork after death (death certificate, bank claims, insurance, property transfer). Low frequency, extreme intensity. A few small startups exist (names to verify).
11. Tuition and coaching discovery in Tier-2/3 with verified results - parents rely on word of mouth. Players: UrbanPro, Superprof.
12. Wedding and event vendors in Tier-2/3 - caterer, tent, decorator, band price and quality opacity. Players: WedMeGood, WeddingWire India (directories, no price or quality guarantee).
13. Local small business going online - kirana, salon, clinic, tailor with no website, no online ordering, no booking. Players: ONDC sellers apps, Dukaan, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business. Founder-fit candidate (SiteBlitz).
14. Farm machinery on demand - tractor, harvester, sprayer by the hour. Players: Mahindra Trringo, EM3 Agri, government custom-hiring-centre apps - possibly tried and died.
