# Agenda - India: unsolved everyday gaps (Zomato / OYO / Uber-class)

1. Rubric: agree the scoring rubric (frequency, intensity, reach, supply fragmentation / trust, willingness to pay, defensibility, speed to test) before ranking anything.
2. Long list: at least 15 everyday pains in India that are still handled offline or by failing apps - challenge, cut or extend Claude's 14 seed hypotheses.
3. Proof of "unsolved": for each surviving gap, who already tried it in India (live / pivoted / dead, with source) and the structural reason they have not won.
4. Top 5: score and rank; for each the person with the pain, product, first wedge (city + segment), supply acquisition, revenue model, biggest risk.
5. How to beat incumbents: which proven lever (aggregation, standardisation, trust layer, price transparency, instant availability, city density, cheaper supply model, ignored wedge) applies to each top-5 idea, and why it would work now.
6. Founder fit and 30-day tests: which top-5 idea this small India team can start first, and the one cheap test (with a pass / fail number) that proves or kills each.
7. Graveyard: ideas that look attractive but are traps (already won by a giant, broken unit economics, or regulatory wall), with the reason.
