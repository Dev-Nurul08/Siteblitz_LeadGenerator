## Definitions for this round
"Totally digital" = the founder works from a phone and laptop in Navsari. No home visits, no delivery, no
stock, no warehouse, no shop, no employed field staff. Customers may be in Navsari, anywhere in India or
abroad. Third parties who deliver a service online (a teacher on video, for example) are allowed if they are
independent and paid per job.

The seven gates from the previous round (an idea must pass all seven):
1 Lawful; 2 Positive transaction economics (money left after direct costs); 3 Identified payer;
4 Consumer-only (the buyer is a household / individual); 5 NOW: zero physical operations;
6 Beginner-executable (the founder can understand and run the pilot personally);
7 Low starting capital (pilot at most Rs 25,000, no payroll, no equipment).
S = 1 (one-person side income) to 5 (national brand) if it works for 5 years.
L = 1 (Navsari base irrelevant) to 5 (Navsari base is the unfair advantage).
