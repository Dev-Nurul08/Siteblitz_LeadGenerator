## OUT OF SCOPE (will NOT build)
- Sending to every country at once, or any country before its own review; listing-only addresses; guessed addresses.
- Gmail / Workspace / Microsoft 365 / SES / SendGrid / Mailgun / Postmark / Brevo as the cold sender.
- Tracking pixels, open counts, "I saw you viewed" messages, link shorteners, click-tracking redirects.
- Synthetic or AI-cloned voice, audio attachments, AMP email, GIFs.
- Automatic sending of any commercial reply (price, sample, scope, discount, date, contract) without the founder's approval.
- WhatsApp sending of any kind (later topic).

- [ ] E0: production pages show no invented testimonial, client name or result, no city other than Gujarat, and a working contact route; screenshots at 1280 and 390.
