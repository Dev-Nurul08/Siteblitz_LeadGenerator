# REFERENCE_MESSAGES - style anchors for the AI writer

Status: DRAFT 2026-11-10, waiting for the founder's `go`. Attach this file to every writer request. The writer matches
their shape and tone; it does not copy their sentences. Words in [brackets] are filled from LEAD / COMPANY_FACTS; a
message that still contains a bracket after writing fails the validator.

Word counts (in brackets after each heading) cover greeting to last sentence, without signature and opt-out line.
They were counted by `docs/outreach/count_words.py`.

Hello [Business Name] team,

I found [Business Name] on Instagram, but I couldn't find a website for it anywhere. A simple page of your own would show clients your services and prices without scrolling through posts.

I'm [Sender] from SiteBlitz, a small web studio in Gujarat. We build websites for local businesses, starting at ₹7,999 (domain and hosting are separate).

Would you like me to send a short outline for [Business Name]?

[Sender]
SiteBlitz | [reply address]
[postal address]
If this isn't useful, reply "stop" and I won't write again.

Why it works: the observation comes first and can be checked in ten seconds (P1). The second sentence names one
effect for their clients (P2). The sender is honest and small (P5), the price is stated once (P4), and the email ends
with one yes/no question about a small thing (P3, P7).
