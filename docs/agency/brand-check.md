## Not checked (needs a browser and a captcha - founders, about 15 minutes, free)
1. IP India public trademark search: https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch -> "Wordmark", type `SITEBLITZ`,
   classes 35 and 42, "Contains". Screenshot the result.
2. EUIPO eSearch and the WIPO Global Brand Database for `SITEBLITZ` (classes 35, 42) - the French company may have
   filed in the EU.
3. USPTO trademark search for `SITEBLITZ` - the plan approaches US agencies from week 3.

## Recommendation
- If any search shows a filed or registered SITEBLITZ mark in classes 35 / 42: **rename now**. There are no clients
  to migrate, and US/EU agencies would find the French company first.
- If all three are clean: keeping the name is a business choice with a known confusion risk abroad; the agency pilot
  in India is little affected (agencies' clients never see the name).
- Either way: decide in one working day, then buy the domain and one mailbox. Do not buy siteblitz.in "because it is
  free" before the decision. The truthful website goes live now regardless (it does not need the decision).
