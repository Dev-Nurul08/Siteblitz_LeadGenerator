# White-label working terms - DRAFT for a lawyer's review

**Not legal advice. Do not sign or send as a contract before a lawyer reviews it** (RESULT rows 6 and 10: counsel
reviews the client-protection wording; a broad non-solicitation clause is not automatically enforceable). Until then,
the quote (`quote-template.md`) carries the commercial terms, and these terms may be attached as "our working terms".
Values in `[ ]` are for the lawyer and founders to set; nothing here invents a number that is not in the offer.

## 1. Parties
[SiteBlitz contracting party: founder name / firm, address, PAN] ("Developer") and [Agency legal name, address]
("Agency").

## 2. Services
The Developer builds the deliverable described in each accepted quote. Each quote is a separate project under these
terms. If a quote and these terms conflict, the quote wins for that project.

## 3. Agency responsibilities
The Agency owns its client relationship and all communication with its client; supplies the approved design (or
chooses an existing demo layout), final copy, images and access; names one approver; and confirms that it has the
rights to every material it supplies.

## 4. Developer responsibilities
Build to the accepted scope; test on phone and desktop; give a staging link; send written updates; reply within one
business day; hand over the source, deployment and access checklist after the balance is paid.

## 5. Dates
Dates count from ready-to-build (all items in the quote's section 2). Late inputs or late feedback move the dates.

## 6. Payment
50 % advance before work; 50 % after written staging approval and before handover. Payment is not conditional on the
Agency's client paying the Agency. Late payment: [lawyer: interest / suspension wording; note MSMED Act s.15 applies
only if the Developer is Udyam-registered - check].

## 7. Changes and acceptance
One consolidated round of changes within scope. Anything new is a change request, priced in writing before work.
Acceptance = the Agency's written approval of staging. [lawyer: deemed-acceptance period, if any].

## 8. Defects
Reproducible failures against the accepted scope reported within 14 calendar days of handover are fixed free.

## 9. Intellectual property
Ownership of the project-specific code and deliverables passes to [the Agency / the Agency's client, as the Agency
directs] when the full fee is paid. Third-party components (frameworks, fonts, images, libraries) stay under their own
licences. The Developer may reuse its general know-how and non-client-specific components.

## 10. Confidentiality
Each party keeps the other's non-public information, and the Agency's client information, confidential during the
project and [lawyer: period] after it.

## 11. Client protection (white-label)
The Developer will not solicit the Agency's clients introduced through the Agency for [lawyer: period], will not
contact them about a project without the Agency's written approval, and will refer any enquiry about an
Agency-commissioned project back to the Agency. No Developer credit on the site; no public portfolio use without the
Agency's written permission. [lawyer: narrow wording; enforceability under Indian Contract Act s.27]
