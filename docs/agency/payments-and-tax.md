# Getting paid - India and abroad (facts as of 2026-11-12; a CA confirms before the first invoice)

Sources: `docs/debate/2026-11-12-clients-without-selling/research-marketplaces.md` section 3 and `02_chatgpt.md` Q2.

## Decide first (founders)
Who is the contracting party: one founder as a sole proprietor (PAN), or a firm? Whose bank / UPI receives money?
Is contract-work income pooled into the studio? (RESULT open points.)

## India (agencies in India)
- Bank transfer or UPI to the contracting party's account. Invoice per payment moment (advance, balance).
- Invoice fields: invoice number, date, supplier name / address / PAN, agency name / address / GSTIN if any, quote
  number, description, amount, "advance 50 %" or "balance 50 %", payment details.
- No GST is charged while not registered. Registration threshold for services in Gujarat is about **aggregate
  turnover** under the same PAN (domestic + exports together), not exports alone (02_chatgpt.md Q2) - CA confirms.
- Agencies may deduct TDS when they pay a contractor or a professional - ask the CA which section and thresholds
  apply, and make sure the agency issues the TDS certificate.

## Abroad (US agencies, from week 3)
| Route | Cost found | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Skydo | $19 per payment up to $2,000, $29 for $2,001-10,000, 0.3 % above; + 18 % GST on the fee; no FX markup; free FIRA | personal PAN accepted (skydo.com/help); account approval needs ID, bank and documents |
| PayPal | 4.4 % + $0.30 per payment, about 3 % FX, 18 % GST on the fee | purpose code mandatory |
| Payoneer | card 2.90 % + $0.49, ACH 1 %, FX 1-4 %, $29.95/year under $6,000 received | |
| Stripe India | invite-only | not available |
| Razorpay international cards | registered business only | not available to an individual |

**Put the collection cost inside the quote.** Two Skydo payments (advance + balance) = 2 x $19 x 1.18 = **$44.84**:
22 % of a $200 job, 9 % of a $500 job (02_chatgpt.md Q2 calculation). Below about $450 per project the fees pass 10 %;
either price above that, take one payment for very small jobs (still before handover), or decline.

## Questions for the CA (one meeting)
1. Registration: sole proprietor or firm; GST registration needed now or at the aggregate-turnover threshold?
2. Invoice wording while unregistered (domestic and export).
3. Export of services without GST registration: documents needed (FIRA / purpose code), and LUT once registered.
4. TDS deducted by Indian agencies: which section, thresholds, how to claim it.
5. Udyam registration: worth it now (MSMED Act payment protection)?
