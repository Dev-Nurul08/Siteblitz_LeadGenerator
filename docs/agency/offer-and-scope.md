## What we sell
White-label website implementation for agencies. The agency owns the client relationship, sales and pricing to its
client, the approved design, the final copy and images, and all client approvals. SiteBlitz develops, tests on phone
and desktop, gives a staging link to the agency, and hands over to the agency. We never contact the agency's client.

## Not included (quoted separately, or declined)
Original branding · open-ended design exploration · copywriting · CMS editing · e-commerce · payments · booking
systems · user accounts · dashboards · complex animation · migration · ongoing maintenance · domain, hosting and
third-party subscriptions (listed separately before the agency agrees).

## Ready-to-build (the delivery clock starts only when all are true)
1. Scope and price accepted in writing (the quote).
2. Approved design, or the chosen demo layout.
3. Final copy and images.
4. Access needed for deployment (or the agreed hand-off format).
5. A named approver at the agency.
6. The 50 % advance received in the bank / UPI record.

Missing inputs mean the project is not ready; we never guess content. Late feedback moves the delivery date and may
need a new slot.

## Payment
50 % advance before any work · 50 % after staging approval, before final handover and source transfer.
Never "we pay when our client pays". For work inside an agency-owned repository from day 1, use prepaid milestones.
Payment status comes only from the bank / UPI / Skydo record, never from "invoice sent".

## Revisions, defects, aftercare
- One consolidated round of changes within the approved scope.
- A changed design direction, an extra page or a new feature is a **change request** (quoted first).
- A reproducible failure to meet the agreed scope is a **defect** and is fixed free.
- 14 calendar days of defect fixes after handover. Not unlimited edits, not maintenance.

## White-label rules
- No SiteBlitz credit on the client's site; no public use of the project without the agency's written permission.
- No contact with the agency's client unless the agency approves in writing; any client enquiry about the project is
  sent back to the agency.
- Third-party licence notices that the law or a licence requires stay in place.
- We do not lie about who built the site if the law requires disclosure.
