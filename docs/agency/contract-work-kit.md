# Contract-work kit - the second founder's cash route (RESULT rows 3 and 13)

Goal: paid development inside an existing team that already has a backlog and someone who owns priorities - so the
founder only builds. Not "build our whole product from an idea and get paid after launch".

## Where to look (02_chatgpt.md Q5)
| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn Jobs | Search "React contract", "Next.js contract", "frontend contractor"; filter Remote / India, last 7 days. Read the hours: many "contract" roles are full-time. |
| Cutshort | Search React / Next.js, filter contractual or contract-to-hire; check each posting for part-time vs full-time. |
| Flexiple | Developer "Find jobs" route; state weekly hours, stack and that you want an existing team. Acceptance is not guaranteed. |

Apply as the individual developer, not as "a two-person studio".

## Rate band (estimate, not proof)
Start at about **₹1,000 per hour**, band **₹800-₹1,200** (₹6,400-₹9,600 for an 8-hour billable day). ChatGPT based this
on CompanyBench (May 2026: ₹6,000-₹10,000/day gross contract pricing for 3-6-year Next.js developers) and Flexiple
(Aug 2026 mid-level model, about ₹1,031/hour). Claude Code did not open those two sources. Always ask: is this the
amount paid to you, or the agency's bill rate? Are meetings and onboarding paid? Which deductions apply?
The ₹550/hour in the plan is the studio's break-even, not a market rate.

## Weekly hours (protects the agency test)
- Before a contract: 4-6 hours a week on search, applications, interviews, small bounded tests.
- With a contract: at most 20 billable hours + 4 hours admin; 16 fixed hours a week stay for agency builds.
- A full-time contract changes the plan: the studio becomes a one-developer delivery business. Decide that together.

## Ask before accepting (every engagement)
1. Who sets priorities and accepts the work?
2. Is there an existing codebase and a backlog of tickets for the next weeks?
3. Guaranteed paid hours per week, and the availability window you must be online?
4. Payment terms (weekly / monthly), currency, invoice to whom?
5. Exclusivity, outside-work, confidentiality and IP clauses - can you still build for agencies?

## Red flags
Unpaid "trial" longer than a few hours · payment only after launch · all-day availability for few paid hours · no
one owns the product decisions · a request to sign away rights to your other work.
